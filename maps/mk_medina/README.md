# mk_medina: Medina

A North African hill town for DM and TDM (24 DM spawns; 14 team spawns per side), under a
hot afternoon sun. Everything is generated by `build.py` with mohkit.

## Layout

- **Souk square** (centre, z 0): dry tiled fountain, palms, market carts. An L-shaped
  arcade runs along the north (pointed arches) and east (round arches) sides. The north
  arcade carries a **roof terrace** (z 192) reached by a stone stair on the square's west
  wall and by a covered passage from the upper town.
- **Market street** (south, x -2048..2048): awnings and cloth canopies; both ends open
  onto the hillside. A covered gate joins it to the square.
- **West quarter**: stepped alley up to the upper town, palm plaza with a well, a house
  interior (door and windows onto the plaza), dog-leg alleys.
- **East quarter**: street with a stair to the upper town, side plaza, second stepped
  alley, and a cafe that cuts the corner to the market street.
- **Upper town** (z 192): long street broken by a covered bridge (sabat) and sandbags;
  mosque court with a riwaq arcade, fountain, palms and a minaret. Its ends open onto the
  hillside too.
- **Hills**: LOD terrain rises on all sides behind the houses; a playerclip ring keeps
  players on the flat apron at the town exits.

Allies spawn in the south half, Axis in the north half. 24 of the 28 are also DM spawns;
the other four (the south gate, the east arcade, the north arcade and the east plaza) are
team-only.

## Look

The tall street walls carry an ornamental cornice (`algiers/algiertrim`) under the roof
edge, rows of beam ends, a string course over the texture seam at z 256 in the lower town,
window decals and the odd wooden balcony (`wall_trim`, `dress_facades`, `balcony`). Sky
fill is `sundiffuse 1.6` with `ambientlight 20 18 16`, plus bracket lanterns in the alleys,
so the shady lanes stay readable.

## Build

```sh
python -m mohkit build maps/mk_medina -q draft
python -m mohkit build maps/mk_medina -q preview --bots 8 --seconds 60
```

MOHlight is forced to one thread (`META["compile"]`): with several threads it
access-violated at `0x00433F3A` on this map in both draft builds. Expect about 30 min for a
draft light and longer for preview on an Apple Silicon Mac through Wine.
