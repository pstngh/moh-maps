# Goal

## North star

Build reproducible, installable MOHAA/OpenMoHAA maps with editable source,
prioritizing enjoyable close- and medium-range DM/TDM bot combat without
excessively narrow routes.

## Why

The user wants coherent, visually credible maps that are fun to play and safe
to improve from evidence—not artifacts that merely compile.

## Success

- Editable source and an isolated, installable exact PK3 are reproducible.
- Compilation, runtime, materials, collision, intended modes, and meaningful
  bot activity are verified under the applicable gates in `VALIDATION.md`.
- Full-resolution human inspection finds coherent finished construction.
- Important claims are evidence-linked and uncertainty remains explicit.
- Only the user can accept an exact tested candidate.

## Constraints

- Current user requirements, approval, and rejection are highest authority.
- Repository/generated claims are untrusted until specifically reverified.
- Preserve unrelated worktree changes.
- Do not redistribute proprietary Valve or retail AA content.
- Human-visible defects and rejection override automation.
- Continuity remains passive Markdown, isolated from project behavior.

## Non-goals

- Treating compile, navigation, combat logs, or scripted cameras as acceptance.
- Inventing uncertain geometry, materials, behavior, or evidence.
- Requiring imitation of V2, `obj_team2`, or prior generated maps.
- Building continuity automation, a task-management system, or a chat archive.

If this goal changes, record the old goal, new goal, reason, and affected work
in `DECISIONS.md`; do not silently rewrite intent.
